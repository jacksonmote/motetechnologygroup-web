What I can help with

Wi-Fi and mesh coverage

Strong, consistent Wi-Fi from the basement to the back porch.

Good fit if: you have dead zones, video calls drop, or you're juggling range extenders.

Home network setup

A clean, secure network with sensible names and passwords, plus a separate network for guests and smart devices.

Good fit if: you've just moved, changed internet providers, or nobody remembers the router password.

Smart devices

Cameras, doorbells, thermostats, speakers and lighting, set up to work together, ideally from one app.

Good fit if: you've collected devices that each need their own app, or some keep going offline.

Home automation

Routines that run themselves: lights at sunset, the thermostat when you leave, alerts when something needs attention.

Good fit if: you want your smart devices to actually save you steps.

Home PCs: custom builds, setup and repair

A new PC built for how you use it, a smooth move from your old computer, or a fix for the one you have.

Good fit if: you want a gaming or workstation build, a slow computer sped up, or a new machine set up properly.

Home office setup and ergonomics

A workspace that's comfortable for long days: monitor height, desk and chair setup, cable management, docking, and reliable video calls.

Good fit if: you work from home and end the day with a sore neck or a tangle of cables.
