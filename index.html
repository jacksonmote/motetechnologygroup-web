What I can help with

Two kinds of help: business work in person or remote, and in-home setup around Lexington.

For businesses

In person around Lexington, or remote anywhere in the US.

Product strategy

Roadmaps, requirements and prioritization that tie what you build to what the business needs.

Typical projects: turning a backlog of requests into a prioritized roadmap, writing requirements a developer can build from, sanity-checking a new product idea.

Systems & workflow review

A clear-eyed look at how your tools and processes fit together, and where the friction is.

Typical projects: finding where data gets re-typed between tools, choosing the right software for a growing team, cleaning up a messy process before automating it.

Websites & office tech

Modern, fast websites and the everyday tech behind a small office: domains, email, Wi-Fi and backups.

Typical projects: replacing a dated website, setting up Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, fixing office Wi-Fi, putting simple backups and security basics in place.

Automation & AI tooling

Practical automation and AI-assisted tools that remove repetitive work without adding risk.

Typical projects: automating a repetitive report or handoff, connecting tools so updates flow on their own, using AI assistants safely with business data.

Fixed-price projects or ongoing monthly support, scoped to fit.
