Services

Product strategy

Turn ideas and requests into a plan your team can build. I help you decide what matters most, define it clearly, and connect it to what the business needs.

Typical projects:

  • Turning a backlog of requests into a prioritized roadmap
  • Writing requirements a developer can build from
  • Sanity-checking a new product idea before you invest in it

What you get: a roadmap or requirements document your team can act on.

Systems and workflow review

A clear-eyed look at how your tools and processes fit together, and where time and data get lost.

Typical projects:

  • Finding where data gets re-typed between tools
  • Choosing the right software for a growing team
  • Cleaning up a messy process before automating it

What you get: a short written assessment with prioritized, practical recommendations.

Websites and office tech

Modern, fast websites and the everyday tech behind a small office.

Typical projects:

  • Replacing a dated website
  • Setting up Google Workspace or Microsoft 365
  • Fixing office Wi-Fi
  • Putting simple backups and security basics in place

What you get: working systems, set up properly, with notes on how to manage them.

Automation and AI tooling

Practical automation and AI-assisted tools that remove repetitive work without adding risk.

Typical projects:

  • Automating a repetitive report or handoff
  • Connecting tools so updates flow on their own
  • Using AI assistants safely with business data

What you get: automations that run reliably, documented so your team can maintain them.
