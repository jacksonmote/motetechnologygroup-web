Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Just ask.

General

What areas do you serve?

Home services cover Lexington and surrounding Bluegrass communities. Business work is available in person around Lexington or remotely anywhere in the US.

How do I get started?

Send an email to hello@motetechnologygroup.com with a short note about what's going on. I'll follow up to set up a quick call.

Is the first conversation free?

Yes. The intro call is free and there's no obligation.

When are you available?

Evening and weekend appointments are available. Remote help can often be scheduled sooner.

Do you work with both Mac and Windows?

Yes. I work with Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, plus the common smart-home platforms.

Home services

How does pricing work?

You get a flat quote before any work starts, based on what we discuss on the intro call. No hourly surprises.

Do I need to buy equipment?

Sometimes. If new equipment would help, I'll recommend specific options that fit your home and budget, and explain why. You can buy it yourself, or I can purchase it for you as part of the quote, and then I set it up.

Will you work with the equipment I already have?

Yes, whenever it makes sense. I'll be honest if something is holding your setup back.

Can you help remotely?

Yes. Many settings changes, app setups and troubleshooting questions can be handled remotely.

What happens after the visit?

I'll walk you through everything and leave simple notes. If you need help later, just reach out.

Do you build custom PCs?

Yes. I can design and build a PC around how you'll use it, set up a new computer, move your files from an old one, or troubleshoot one that's slow or acting up.

Business services

What size businesses do you work with?

Mostly small businesses and growing teams, from early-stage companies to established local offices.

How is business work priced?

Either as a fixed-price project with a defined scope, or as ongoing monthly support. Every engagement starts with a written proposal so the scope and price are clear up front.

Do you work in person or remotely?

Both. In person around Lexington, or remote anywhere in the US.

Will my team be able to manage things after you're done?

That's the goal. Every engagement ends with documentation and a handoff so your team isn't dependent on me.

Have a project in mind?

Send me an email or a message on LinkedIn and let's see if it's a fit.
